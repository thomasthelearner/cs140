Pictures

  • What kind of photos are good to encode using JPEG? Photos, which is lossy, with lots of colors and gradients, such as photographs - real-life images, are well-suited for JPEG encoding.
  • What kind of photos are good to encode using GIF? Photos, which is lossy, with limited colors, short, looping animation or low-resolution with the sharp edges (such as icons/buttons/logos) are well-suited for GIF encoding.
  • What is unique about the PNG format? PNG supports lossless compression and transparency, making it ideal for images that require high quality and clear backgrounds.
  • How do you resize your photo to the size it should be displayed? And Why should you resize photos using photo editing software instead of resizing it using CSS? You can use image editing software or online tools to resize your photo to the desired dimensions before displaying it on your website or using CSS to resize it, but it will consume more memory and load time.
